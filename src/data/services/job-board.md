## See Every Job at a Glance

Our **visual job board** lets you track every vehicle from customer drop-off to pickup. Drag and drop jobs between columns as work progresses. No more sticky notes, no more guessing.

![Service images](/images/ns-img-397.png)

## What's included

Everything you need to manage your shop floor efficiently.

- **Visual Kanban board** - See all jobs organized by status (Waiting, In Progress, Complete).
- **Drag and drop** - Move jobs between columns as work progresses.
- **Job details** - Customer info, vehicle, services, and notes in one place.
- **Technician assignment** - Assign jobs to specific techs with one click.
- **Status tracking** - Know exactly where every vehicle stands.
- **Priority flagging** - Mark urgent jobs so they stand out.

![Service images](/images/ns-img-398.png)

## Use cases

Perfect for shops that want to eliminate chaos and know exactly what's happening at all times.

- **Daily workflow** - Start each day knowing exactly what needs attention.
- **Team coordination** - Techs see their assigned jobs without asking.
- **Customer updates** - Answer "Where's my car?" instantly.
- **Bottleneck detection** - Spot jobs stuck waiting on parts or approval.
- **End of day review** - See what got done and what carries over.

Whether you have 2 bays or 20, the job board scales with your operation.

- **Real-time updates** across all devices.
