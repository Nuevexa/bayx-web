## Never Miss an Update

Get **instant notifications** when important things happen. Estimate approved? Job complete? Part arrived? Know immediately without checking the dashboard.

![Service images](/images/ns-img-397.png)

## What's included

Stay informed without constant monitoring.

- **Estimate approvals** - Know the moment a customer says yes.
- **Job status changes** - Track progress in real time.
- **Technician updates** - See when work is complete.
- **Customer messages** - Get notified of incoming communications.
- **Custom alerts** - Choose what you want to be notified about.
- **Multi-channel** - Browser, mobile, and email options.

![Service images](/images/ns-img-398.png)

## Use cases

Perfect for owners and managers who need to stay informed while doing other things.

- **Offsite monitoring** - Know what's happening from anywhere.
- **Quick response** - Answer customer approvals immediately.
- **Workflow management** - React to status changes promptly.
- **Team coordination** - Keep everyone synchronized.
- **Priority handling** - Never miss urgent updates.

## Built for busy people

Information comes to you, not the other way around.

- **Push notifications** on mobile devices.
- **Desktop alerts** in your browser.
- **Email digests** for summary updates.
- **Customizable** by notification type.
- **Do not disturb** modes when needed.
