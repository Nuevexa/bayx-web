## Build Estimates in Seconds

Create a **pre-configured service menu** with your common repairs and maintenance items. Pull from your menu to build estimates instantly.

![Service images](/images/ns-img-397.png)

## What's included

Template your most common services.

- **Custom service items** - Add your own services with pricing.
- **Labor time presets** - Set expected hours for each service.
- **Parts templates** - Include standard parts in templates.
- **Category organization** - Group services by type.
- **Quick estimate builder** - Pull from menu to create quotes fast.
- **Flexible pricing** - Adjust per customer if needed.

![Service images](/images/ns-img-398.png)

## Use cases

Perfect for shops that want consistent pricing and faster quoting.

- **Oil changes** - Standard pricing, one click to add.
- **Brake jobs** - Include pads, rotors, labor all preset.
- **Maintenance packages** - Bundle common services together.
- **Inspections** - Standardize your inspection process.
- **Custom work** - Still flexible for unique jobs.
