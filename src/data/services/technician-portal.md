## Empower Your Technicians

Give each tech their own mobile portal accessible from any phone browser. They see assigned jobs, log time, upload photos, and update status without leaving their bay.

![Service images](/images/ns-img-397.png)

## What's included

Everything your techs need to work efficiently.

- Personal login - Each tech has their own secure access.
- Job assignments - See what's assigned to them.
- Time clock - Clock in/out and track time per job.
- Photo upload - Document findings and completed work.
- Status updates - Mark jobs as in progress or complete.
- Notes - Add observations and recommendations.

![Service images](/images/ns-img-398.png)

## Use cases

Perfect for shops that want accountability and documentation without hassle.

- Morning assignments - Techs see their workload when they clock in.
- Photo documentation - Capture issues before and after repair.
- Time accuracy - Know exactly how long each job takes.
- Accountability - Track who worked on what.
- Paperless operation - No clipboards, no lost paperwork.

![Service images](/images/ns-img-dark-217.jpg)

Works on any smartphone, no app download needed.
