## Welcome to BayX

BayX is a profitability-first shop management system designed for small independent garages (1-5 staff). This guide will walk you through setting up your account and getting started.

## Step 1: Create Your Account

1. Visit the BayX signup page
2. Enter your email address and create a password
3. Verify your email by clicking the link sent to your inbox
4. Complete your garage profile with your shop name and address

## Step 2: Choose Your Plan

BayX offers three pricing tiers:
- Basic Bay: For solo operators - 1 bay, essential features
- Pro Bay: For growing shops - up to 3 bays
- Fleet Master: For larger operations - unlimited bays

All plans include a 14-day free trial.

## Step 3: Set Up Your Garage

After selecting your plan, you'll be guided through:
1. Adding your garage details (name, address, contact info)
2. Configuring your service bays
3. Setting up your service menu
4. Inviting your technicians

## Step 4: Explore Your Dashboard

Your dashboard is the command center of your shop. From here you can:
- View all active jobs on the Kanban board
- Create new jobs with Quick Intake
- Monitor profitability metrics
- Track technician efficiency
