## What are Service Bays?

Service bays represent the physical work spaces in your garage. Each bay can have one job active at a time, helping you manage capacity and workflow.

## Setting Up Bays

1. Go to **Settings** > **Bays**
2. Click **Add Bay**
3. Enter a name for the bay (e.g., "Bay 1", "Lift Station A")
4. Optionally add a description
5. Click **Save**

## Bay Limits by Plan

The number of bays you can create depends on your subscription:
- **Basic Bay**: 1 bay
- **Pro Bay**: Up to 3 bays
- **Fleet Master**: Unlimited bays

## Assigning Jobs to Bays

When creating or editing a job:
1. Look for the **Bay Assignment** dropdown
2. Select an available bay
3. The job will be tracked against that bay

## Bay Capacity

BayX helps you understand capacity:
- Bays with active jobs are marked as "In Use"
- Available bays are shown as "Open"
- You can see at a glance how many bays are occupied

## Best Practices

- Name bays consistently (e.g., "Bay 1", "Bay 2" or "Alignment", "Oil Change", "General")
- Consider specialized bays for specific services
- Monitor bay utilization to understand if you need to hire more staff or expand
