## Creating a New Job

There are two ways to create a job in BayX:

### Method 1: Quick Intake (Fastest)

The Quick Intake widget on your dashboard lets you create jobs in seconds:

1. Enter the customer's phone number - if they're an existing customer, their info auto-fills
2. Add the customer's name (first and last)
3. Enter vehicle details (year, make, model, license plate)
4. Describe the issue or service requested
5. Click Create Job

The job is immediately added to the "Intake" column on your Kanban board.

### Method 2: Full Job Form

For more detailed job creation, click "New Job" from the operations page:

1. Select or create a customer
2. Add vehicle information
3. Add line items (services, parts, labor)
4. Assign a technician (optional)
5. Set the estimated completion date
6. Save the job

## Adding Line Items

Every job can have multiple line items:

- Labor: Hours × your labor rate
- Parts: Part cost with markup
- Services: Pre-configured service packages from your menu
- Supply Fee: Shop supplies (consumables)

## Assigning a Technician

Click on a job card and select a technician from the dropdown. The job will appear in their Tech Portal immediately.

## What's Next?

After creating a job, you can:
- Generate an estimate for customer approval
- Move it through the workflow as work progresses
- Share the tracking link with the customer
