## What is Service Profitability?

Service profitability shows you which types of repairs and services generate the highest margins for your shop. Not all services are equally profitable!

## Finding Service Margins

Check the Profit Pulse sidebar on your dashboard. The Service Margin section shows your top service categories ranked by profit margin.

## How Service Margin is Calculated

For each service category:

```
Margin = (Revenue - Costs) ÷ Revenue × 100%
```

Where Costs include:
- Parts cost
- Labor cost (Actual Hours × Technician Cost Rate)
- Supplies

## Service Categories

BayX tracks margins for:
- Oil: Oil changes and related services
- Brakes: Brake pad, rotor, and line work
- Tires: Tire sales, rotation, balancing
- Engine: Engine repair and maintenance
- Diagnostic: Inspection and diagnostic services
- Other: All other services

## Reading the Report

| Margin | Performance |
|--------|-------------|
| 60%+ | Excellent - focus on these |
| 40-59% | Good - room to improve |
| Below 40% | Review pricing and costs |

## Why This Matters

Understanding service profitability helps you:
- Focus marketing on high-margin services
- Identify services that need pricing adjustments
- Know when to decline low-margin work
- Train staff on profitable upsells

## Improving Low-Margin Services

1. Increase prices: market will often bear more
2. Find cheaper parts suppliers: reduce cost
3. Improve efficiency: bill more hours per job
4. Bundle services: increase ticket size
