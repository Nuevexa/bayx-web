## What is the Tracking Link?

Every job in BayX has a unique tracking link that you can share with customers. This link allows them to:
- View their vehicle's current status
- See uploaded photos from technicians
- Approve estimates
- Check progress without calling your shop

## How to Share the Link

1. Open the job from your Kanban board
2. In the job details, find the Copy Tracking Link button
3. Click the button - the link is copied to your clipboard
4. Paste the link into WhatsApp, SMS, or email

## What Customers See

When a customer opens their tracking link, they'll see:

### Status Progress Bar
A visual indicator showing where their job is in the process:
Intake → Waiting Approval → In Progress → Ready

### Vehicle Details
- Year, make, model
- License plate
- Current technician assigned

### Photos
Any photos uploaded by technicians during inspection or repair

### Estimate (if applicable)
If the job is in "Waiting Approval" status, customers see:
- Full estimate breakdown
- Total cost
- Approve Work button

## Security

The tracking link is:
- Read-only - customers cannot edit anything
- Secure - uses a unique 32-character token
- Private - only accessible with the link

## Tips

- Share the link immediately after creating the job
- Send updates when status changes
- Use WhatsApp or SMS for fastest delivery
- Include a personal message: "Hi [Name], here's your car's status!"
