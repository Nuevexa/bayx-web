## Customer Profiles

BayX maintains a database of your customers. Each customer profile stores:
- Contact information (name, phone, email)
- Vehicle history
- Job history
- Communication preferences

## Adding New Customers

Customers are automatically created when you:
1. Use Quick Intake with a new phone number
2. Create a job with customer details not in the system

You can also manually add customers:
1. Go to the customer management area
2. Click Add Customer
3. Enter their details
4. Save the profile

## Customer Information

### Basic Details
- First and last name
- Phone number (used for lookup)
- Email address (for digital communications)
- Address (optional, for records)

### Vehicles
Each customer can have multiple vehicles:
- Year, Make, Model
- License plate
- VIN (optional)
- Color (helpful for identification)

### Job History
View all past and current jobs for the customer, including:
- Dates of service
- Services performed
- Total spent

## Finding Customers

Use the search function with:
- Phone number (most reliable)
- Customer name
- License plate

## Customer Lookup in Quick Intake

When entering a phone number in Quick Intake:
1. Type the phone number
2. If the customer exists, their name and vehicles auto-populate
3. Select the vehicle or add a new one
4. Continue with the job
