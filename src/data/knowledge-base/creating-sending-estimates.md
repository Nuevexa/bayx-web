## What is an Estimate?

An estimate is a detailed quote that you send to customers before starting work. It lists all services, parts, and labor costs so the customer can approve the work.

## Creating an Estimate

1. Open a job from the Kanban board
2. Click the Estimates tab in the job details
3. Add line items:
   - Services from your service menu
   - Individual parts with costs
   - Labor hours at your shop rate
   - Supply fees
4. Review the total
5. Click Generate Estimate

## Estimate Line Items

Each line item includes:
- Description: What service or part is being provided
- Quantity: Number of units (hours, parts, etc.)
- Unit Price: Price per unit
- Total: Quantity × Unit Price

## Sending the Estimate

Once generated, you can share the estimate with the customer:

1. Click Copy Tracking Link on the job details page
2. Paste the link into WhatsApp, SMS, or email
3. The customer can view the estimate and approve directly

## Customer Approval Process

When a customer receives the tracking link, they'll see:
- Their vehicle details
- The estimate breakdown
- Total cost
- Approve Work button

When they click Approve, the job automatically moves to "Waiting Parts" or "In Progress" status.

## Tips for Better Estimates

- Be detailed in descriptions - customers appreciate transparency
- Group related services together
- Always include supply fees separately
- Follow up if no response within 24 hours
