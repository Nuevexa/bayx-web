## What is the Tech Portal?

The Tech Portal is a mobile-optimized view designed specifically for technicians. It's simple, focused, and distraction-free.

## Accessing the Tech Portal

1. Technicians receive an invitation email from the shop owner
2. Click the link to create an account
3. Log in with email and password
4. The Tech Portal loads automatically

## What Technicians See

### Job List
A simple list of job cards assigned to you, showing:
- Vehicle: Year, Make, Model
- License Plate
- Task List: Services to perform
- Status: Current job status

### Job Details
Tap a job to see:
- Full service list
- Customer notes (issue description)
- Any photos already uploaded

## Key Actions

### Clock In / Clock Out
Big buttons at the top of each job:
- Clock In: Start tracking time on this job
- Clock Out: Stop tracking time

Time is automatically logged for efficiency calculations.

### Upload Photos
- Tap Upload Photo to take a picture
- Photos are attached to the job
- Customers can see photos on their tracking link
- Great for documenting issues found during inspection

### Mark Complete
When finished with a job:
- Tap Mark Complete
- The job moves to "Ready" status
- The owner is notified

## What Technicians Cannot See

To keep the interface simple and focused:
- No pricing information
- No profit margins
- No customer phone numbers
- No access to settings or other jobs
