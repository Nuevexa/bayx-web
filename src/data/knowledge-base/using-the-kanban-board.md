## What is the Kanban Board?

The Kanban board is a visual workflow management tool that shows all your active jobs organized by status. Each column represents a stage in the repair process.

## Workflow Columns

### Intake
Jobs that have just been received. The customer has brought their vehicle in, but no work has started yet.

### Waiting Approval
An estimate has been generated and sent to the customer. The job is on hold until the customer approves the work.

### Waiting Parts
The customer has approved the estimate, but required parts need to be ordered or received before work can begin.

### In Progress
A technician is actively working on the vehicle. The clock is running!

### Ready
The repair is complete. The vehicle is ready for the customer to pick up.

### Completed
The job has been paid and closed. The customer has picked up their vehicle.

## Moving Jobs Between Columns

Simply drag and drop a job card from one column to another to update its status. The change is saved instantly and reflected everywhere.

## Job Card Information

Each job card shows:
- Customer name
- Vehicle (year, make, model)
- Current technician assignment
- Total estimated value
- Time in current status

## Tips for Workflow Efficiency

1. Process Intake quickly: Jobs shouldn't sit in Intake for more than a few hours
2. Follow up on Waiting Approval: Use the tracking link to remind customers
3. Monitor Waiting Parts: This column often reveals supply chain issues
4. Celebrate Ready: This is where you make money - get those pickups scheduled!
