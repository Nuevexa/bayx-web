## Common Login Issues

### "Invalid email or password"

**Cause**: Email or password is incorrect.

**Solutions**:
1. Double-check your email for typos
2. Make sure Caps Lock is off
3. Try the **Forgot Password** option
4. Check if you're using the correct account (Owner vs. Technician)

### "Account not found"

**Cause**: No account exists with that email.

**Solutions**:
1. Check if you used a different email to sign up
2. If you received an invitation, click the link to create your account first
3. Contact your shop owner if you're a technician

### "Email not verified"

**Cause**: You haven't clicked the verification link.

**Solutions**:
1. Check your inbox for the verification email
2. Check your spam/junk folder
3. Click **Resend Verification Email** on the login page
4. Wait a few minutes and check again

### "Account suspended"

**Cause**: Subscription issue or admin action.

**Solutions**:
1. Contact your shop owner (if technician)
2. Check if your payment method is valid (if owner)
3. Contact BayX support

## Resetting Your Password

1. Go to the login page
2. Click **Forgot Password**
3. Enter your email address
4. Check your inbox for reset link
5. Click the link and create a new password

## Still Having Issues?

If you've tried these solutions and still can't log in:
1. Clear your browser cache and cookies
2. Try a different browser
3. Try an incognito/private window
4. Contact BayX support at support@bayx.io
