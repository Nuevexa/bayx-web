## What is Effective Labor Rate?

Effective Labor Rate (ELR) is the actual average rate you're earning per billed hour. It often differs from your posted door rate due to discounts, flat-rate pricing, and bundled services.

**Formula:**
```
ELR = Total Labor Revenue ÷ Total Billed Hours
```

## Example

If your shop:
- Generated $8,800 in labor revenue this week
- Billed 80 hours of labor

Your ELR is: $8,800 ÷ 80 = $110/hour

## Where to Find ELR

Look at the top metrics bar on your dashboard. ELR is displayed alongside Today's Revenue and Pending Approvals.

## Why ELR Matters

Your **door rate** might be $120/hour, but if your ELR is $95/hour, you're effectively giving away 21% in discounts.

ELR reveals:
- How much you're actually earning per hour
- Whether discounts are hurting profitability
- If flat-rate pricing is working for or against you

## What the Numbers Mean

| ELR vs. Door Rate | Status | Action |
|-------------------|--------|--------|
| ELR ≥ Door Rate | Excellent | Maintain pricing |
| ELR = 90-99% | Good | Minor adjustments |
| ELR < 90% | Warning | Review discounting practices |

## How to Improve ELR

1. **Reduce discounts**: Stick to posted rates
2. **Review flat-rate jobs**: Make sure you're not underpricing
3. **Upsell diagnostics**: Charge for inspection time
4. **Track parts markup**: Don't absorb parts costs
5. **Bill for actual time**: If jobs take longer, adjust estimates

## Using ELR for Decisions

- Compare ELR across different service types
- Track ELR trends over weeks and months
- Set ELR targets for your team
