## Adding Staff Members

As the shop owner, you can invite technicians to join your BayX account. They'll get their own login and access to the Tech Portal.

## How to Send an Invite

1. Go to **Settings** > **Staff**
2. Click **Invite Staff Member**
3. Enter the technician's:
   - Email address
   - Name
   - Role (Technician)
   - Hourly cost rate (for profitability calculations)
   - Hourly bill rate (what you charge customers)
4. Click **Send Invitation**

The technician will receive an email with a link to create their account.

## Staff Roles

BayX has two roles:

### Owner (You)
- Full access to everything
- Creates jobs, manages finances, views profitability
- Can access both dashboard and tech portal

### Technician
- Mobile-optimized Tech Portal only
- Can view assigned jobs
- Can clock in/out
- Can upload photos
- Cannot see pricing, margins, or customer phone numbers

## Setting Cost and Bill Rates

**Cost Rate**: What you pay the technician per hour
- Example: $35/hour

**Bill Rate**: What you charge customers for their labor
- Example: $110/hour

These rates are crucial for calculating true job profitability.

## Managing Staff

From **Settings > Staff**, you can:
- View all staff members
- Edit their details
- Deactivate accounts
- Resend invitations
